Log file

Uninstalling Tof deletes its own storage. A copy of the log in a folder you choose, like Documents, stays on the phone. After reinstalling, choose the same folder to bring the log back.

Media folder

Tof copies every picture and video it saves into the folder you choose, named by date and contact, and keeps adding new ones. The copies stay if Tof is uninstalled. To save just one, open it and tap Save a copy.
